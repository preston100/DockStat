# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first public release

- Glass panels that sit right beside the Dock, matching its height and style.
- Stats: CPU, memory, chip temperature (Apple Silicon), battery, network, disk.
- Drag-and-drop layout: choose which stats sit on each side of the Dock.
- Click a panel for details: 2-minute graphs, per-core CPU, memory pressure and breakdown,
  battery health, cycles and power draw, disk space.
- Four tile styles: Numbers, Bars, Rings, Graph. °C or °F. Update every 1–5 seconds.
- Works with the Dock at the bottom, left or right, auto-hide, full-screen apps and
  multiple displays.
- Optionally resizes the Dock to make room, and can put it back when DockStat quits.
- Welcome window that explains the Accessibility permission before asking for it.
- Launch at login, daily update check (can be turned off), VoiceOver labels.
- Universal app (Apple Silicon and Intel), macOS 14 or later; built and tested on macOS 26+.
